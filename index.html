# Games Radio – Privacy Policy **Last updated: November 10, 2026** Games Radio (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Games Radio, how it is used, and what choices you have. ## 1. Who is responsible for the App? Games Radio is an independently developed application. For privacy questions, requests concerning your personal data, or account deletion, contact: **Email:** gamesradiosupport@gmail.com ## 2. Information we process Depending on how you use the App, the following information may be processed: ### Account information If you create an account, we use your email address and authentication information to register your account, sign you in, verify your email address, and protect access to your account. Account authentication is provided by Supabase. Your password is handled by Supabase authentication services and is not available to us in readable form. ### Information stored on your device The App stores certain preferences locally on your device, including your favorite stations and recent search text. This information is used to provide App functionality and is not currently stored in our Supabase database. ### Advertising and technical information The App integrates Google AdMob. The current configuration uses test advertisements. When the advertising SDK operates, Google may process technical and advertising-related information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information. The information processed may depend on your device settings, consent choices, and the advertising configuration. For more information, please review Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy ### Radio streaming When you play a station, your device connects to a third-party radio stream provider. That provider may receive technical connection information, such as your IP address, as part of delivering the audio stream. These providers operate independently and are responsible for their own privacy practices. ## 3. How we use information We use information to: - Create and manage user accounts. - Authenticate users and protect account access. - Provide radio playback, favorites, search, and App settings. - Display advertisements. - Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability and security of the App. - Respond to privacy requests and support messages. ## 4. How information is shared We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the following service providers when needed to operate the App: - **Supabase:** account registration, authentication, and related backend services. - **Google AdMob / Google:** advertising services and related technical processing. - **Third-party radio stream providers:** delivery of the radio streams you choose to play. These providers process information under their own privacy terms and applicable service agreements. ## 5. Data storage and security Account authentication data is processed by Supabase. The App also stores certain preferences locally on your device. We use services and technical measures intended to protect information. Connections to supported services use encrypted transport where provided by those services. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 6. Data retention and deletion We retain account information while your account remains active or as needed to provide the App. You can request account deletion through the account deletion option in the App's Settings or by contacting gamesradiosupport@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your Supabase authentication account. Favorites and search information stored locally on your device are separate from the account and may remain on the device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Some limited technical records may be retained by service providers for security, legal, or operational purposes in accordance with their own retention policies. ## 7. Your privacy rights Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise your rights, contact gamesradiosupport@gmail.com. ## 8. Children's privacy Games Radio is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can review the request. ## 9. Third-party services The App relies on third-party services to provide authentication, advertising, and radio streaming. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies. ## 10. Changes to this Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at the public Privacy Policy webpage, together with its updated date. ## 11.
